Expected (producer) = the total announced by the Creaves instance at its last full resync (stored on this console). Received = distinct animals seen in received events. Confirmed = latest received state hash matches the stored animal. Unconfirmed = stale or missing state. The "matches producer" badge appears only when the stored-animal checksum is non-empty AND equals the producer's announced expected checksum — that is the completeness proof. An empty database shows "no data" and can never count as a match.
